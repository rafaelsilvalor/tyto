TYTO-225: the built-in templates no longer carry a brand's logo or signature. Each draws its brand kit's (ADR 0063) and, where no kit supplies one, a placeholder in the same box and style: an invented rounded mark for the logo and `@assinatura` for the signature (ADR 0065). The three `banner-roxo` backgrounds lose the logo painted into them, and the banner draws the kit's logo, or the placeholder, where it stood. The drawn nodes are named `logo` and `signature`.
