`huggedText` lays text out as wide as its widest line up to a limit, and `paddedCard` draws a padded, rounded, optionally shadowed surface around any block, padding from its box or its capitals (`HUGGED_TEXT_PARAMETERS`, `PADDED_CARD_PARAMETERS`); `labelValueRuns` writes `Label: value` facts as runs in two styles (`LABEL_VALUE_PARAMETERS`); and a `titleBlock` text part may say `measured` to take its measured height (TYTO-890).
