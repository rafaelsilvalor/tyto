TYTO-200: three new templates, `simulados-semana-ec`, `simulados-semana-ecj` and `simulados-semana-oab` — the weekly mock-exam agenda of Estratégia Concursos (stories only), Carreira Jurídica and OAB, one composition in three accents. Each `::lamina` is one slide: `Domingo 26/10 | Aplicação às 08h30 & correção às 14h` starts a day and every line with no `|` under it is one of its exams. The title is on every slide; the call to comment is on the last grid only. `pillTable` gains a group `caption`, the band under a heading drawn from the heading line's second field.
