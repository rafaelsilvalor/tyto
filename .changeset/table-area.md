The tables take an area and a parameter schema (TYTO-891): `tableIn` and `slotTableIn` with `TABLE_PARAMETERS` and `SLOT_TABLE_PARAMETERS`, a number given as a parameter laid over the style's own (`tableStyleWith`, `slotTableStyleWith`), returning their overflow. `table` and `slotTable` draw the same bytes as before.
