TYTO-247: the window opens and edits `.txt` and `.md` files in a tab. A click or Enter on one in the Explorer opens it, a new one made there opens too, and File ▸ Open offers "Text files" and "All supported files" after "Brief". A text tab is plain text: no preview, no export and no export presets; the status bar says "Plain text" or "Markdown", and Problems says text files are not checked. Saving, the unsaved dot and hot exit work as for a brief, and Save As offers the tab's own extension.
