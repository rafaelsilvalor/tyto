TYTO-279: an **Explorer** panel in the left area, above the queue, lists the open workspace folder. Opening a folder with File ▸ Open Folder opens it, clicking a brief opens it in a tab, and a file made or deleted outside Tyto shows within a moment. Its status-bar button and **Show or hide: Explorer** in the command bar show and hide it (ADR 0115).
