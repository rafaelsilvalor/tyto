TYTO-323: the preview has two modes, **One slide** and **All slides**. All slides shows every slide of the chosen format in a grid, in order and numbered, scaled so they all fit; clicking one opens it as the main slide in One slide mode. Two buttons in the preview's bar switch the mode, as do the commands `preview.showOneSlide` and `preview.showAllSlides`, and each preview tab remembers its mode across a reload and a quit.
