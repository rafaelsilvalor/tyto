The command panel completes as you type (TYTO-496): commands, options and their values, read off the CLI's own tree, with the window's templates and aliases, the chosen template's formats, the output types, the installed plugins and the open folder's files. Tab takes a suggestion, the arrows choose and Enter takes the chosen one. **Commands: Run…** puts the caret on the prompt, and **Commands: Render This Brief** writes `render` and the brief in front on it. The user manual has a Commands page in both editions.
