TYTO-860: `buttonRow`, one component for a call to action — a row of pills, each filled or
outlined, holding an optional leading icon, its words and an optional end cap. Four
single-colour icons drawn for the kit (`HEART_ICON`, `BALLOON_ICON`, `PAPER_PLANE_ICON`,
`ARROW_ICON`) through `icon`, coloured by the caller, and `capitals` for words set in
capitals.
