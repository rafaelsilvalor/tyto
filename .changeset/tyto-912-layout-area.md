Add `area` and `areaIn`: a layout area that groups children in a column or a row, with gaps,
padding, justify and align, sizes each child fixed, by its content or by a weighted share of
the room left, floats children above the flow, collapses when empty, and takes the brief's
order. `stackIn`, `rowIn` and `insetIn` are now shortcuts over it and draw the same bytes.
